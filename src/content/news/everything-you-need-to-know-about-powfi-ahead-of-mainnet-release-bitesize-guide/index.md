**The official mainnet launch of Powfi is rapidly approaching.** 

We appreciate your excitement and applaud your patience. Getting this right is critical, so we cannot afford to rush the launch or overlook even the smallest detail. Fortunately, we are very close, otherwise we wouldn’t be publishing this pre-launch explainer.

As Alephium’s Core dApp, Powfi is going to activate Phase Two of our roadmap, what we have called “**Aligned Economic**s”. After almost nine months of development, including an intensive community-driven testnet phase, we are determined to deliver secure, institutional-grade DeFi to our Proof-of-Work network.

So, ahead of mainnet launch, here is everything you need to know about how this protocol-owned liquidity engine will reshape the Alephium ecosystem. We’ve made this a bitesize guide, keeping it to important bullet points, rather than endless paragraphs of text. This should make everything we are building and shipping much more digestible.

## What Powfi Will Bring to the Ecosystem

* Powfi will combine a high-performance Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM) DEX with Constant Product Market Maker (CPMM) pools.
* The dApp will introduce seamless liquid staking through the xALPH token.
* Every smart contract component is going to be powered by Ralph, our purpose-built programming language, built natively on the stateful UTXO (sUTXO) model.
* The launch is going to mark a shift toward a self-reinforcing economic flywheel where every trade, stake, and interaction will strengthen the $ALPH coin.
* The application will arrive battle-tested, offering comprehensive DeFi features alongside a polished interface and seamless wallet integrations.

## The Aligned Ecosystem Loop Explained

* Powfi is going to solve the structural fragmentation that traditionally keeps value trapped at the application layer.
* Transaction fees will be captured at the application layer and funneled directly back to the base layer in a transparent, on-chain manner.
* These captured fees will be automatically allocated to $ALPH buybacks and token burns (50%).
* The remaining transaction fees are going to be distributed as staking rewards to long-term participants (50%).
* This unified economic model will ensure that application-layer success and base-layer strength are finally aligned.

## Benefits for Liquidity Providers and Users

* The CLMM design will allow liquidity providers to focus their capital within custom price ranges that they choose.
* This intentional design is going to translate into higher capital efficiency, more predictable returns, and better fee earnings.
* Early liquidity providers, including Coinstancy, are going to deploy capital to signal strong conviction ahead of and during the launch.
* Through single-sided $ALPH liquid staking, users will finally be able to stake their $ALPH and earn staking rewards.
* Stakers are going to be able to maintain asset usability by continuing to trade or access pools using xALPH.

## A Composable Foundation for Developers

* Powfi is going to function as a complete, open-source DeFi primitive.
* We will provide comprehensive SDKs for pricing, routing, and liquidity provisioning.
* Third-party developers will gain direct access to deep, protocol-owned liquidity.
* Ecosystem builders are no longer going to need to bootstrap their own liquidity from scratch or navigate misaligned incentives.
* Other applications will be able to seamlessly integrate with Powfi to accelerate ecosystem expansion through shared economic incentives.

## Our Core Values in Action

* Powfi will remain decentralized, fully open-source, permissionless, and governed by transparent on-chain rules.
* Supported by our BlockFlow sharding algorithm, our highly scalable network is going to continue delivering over 20,000 transactions per second (when needed) while preserving a single-chain user experience.
* The new Aligned Ecosystem Loop will create economic incentives that reward long-term participation rather than fleeting speculation.

## Final Message

Powfi continues on Public Testnet, but will soon be accessible on Mainnet at <https://powfi.alephium.org> - keep an eye out for official comms.

It is our intention to deliver a truly sustainable and secure DeFi ecosystem that lets the entire network grow in true alignment.


Phase Two is about to begin, and **the best is yet to come**.
